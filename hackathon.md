Welcome to the RevenueCat Shipaton — the mobile hackathon that's all about shipping… a ton.
At RevenueCat, we're all about supporting developers to build and monetize amazing apps. After the overwhelming success of last year's Shipaton, we're thrilled to announce the return of our global hackathon — and this year, we're going bigger than ever.

This August and September, we're inviting builders from all corners of the globe to ship new apps and compete for over $700,000 in cash prizes and truly unique rewards — over $1 million worth of prizes in total.



The challenge
Ship a brand-new app to the App Store, Google Play Store, or — new this year — the Samsung Galaxy Store, between August 1st and September 30th, 2026.
Integrate the RevenueCat SDK to power at least one in-app purchase or serve ads through RevenueCat Ads.
Our goal is to give you that final push to ship that project you've had in the back of your mind but never took the time to get started.

Note: Updates to previously released apps won't qualify - it's all about bringing something new to life! You can brainstorm, build, and post before August 1st, but your app must be released for the first time during the Shipaton window.

Categories


Grand Prize: Goes to the app that gains the most user traction and growth momentum during the event. We want to hear about what you've done post-release to push your app growth to the next level.
[NEW] Next Gen Award: A student-only category for the best app submitted by active students — high school, university, bootcamp, campus dev club, anyone learning the craft. Judged on a video submission and open-source code, so no paid Apple or Google developer account is required. Learn more on the Next Gen page.
[NEW] Catvertising Award: Celebrates the most creative and effective use of RevenueCat Ads as a monetization method. We're looking for clever placements, smart integration with the rest of the revenue stack, and an experience users don't hate.
[NEW] Best Game Award: For the best mobile game shipped during the event. We're looking for great gameplay, art direction, and a monetization fit that suits the genre.
#BuildInPublic Award: For developers who share the most interesting development journey on social media. We're looking for compelling lessons learned or clever ideas that came from engaging with the community.
HAMM Award (Help Apps Make Money): RevenueCat exists to "Help Apps Make More Money". This award goes to the project that designs the most robust and creative monetization strategy.
RevenueCat Design Award: For the app that best represents the craft of app development — regardless of business potential. We're looking for innovative ideas and/or beautiful app design and animations.
RevenueCat Peace Prize: Awarded to the project that provides the greatest social good. We're looking for apps with big benefits to communities or society at large.
Conflict of Interest Award: A special category for RevenueCat employees and sponsors who couldn't resist joining the fun. No cash prizes, but a shot at glory.


Influencer Award — Productivity: Christopher Lawley
For a polished productivity app that gives Apple power users one fast, focused place to save and retrieve reusable text snippets, documents, files, and images. Judges will consider product focus, speed, organization, intelligent features, and the quality of the overall design.
Influencer Award — Nutrition & Healthy Eating: Abbey’s Kitchen
For a flexible nutrition app inspired by Abbey’s Hunger Crushing Combo framework. Judges are looking for a compassionate, practical experience that helps users create more satisfying meals in real-life situations without calorie counting, macro tracking, or restrictive meal plans.
Influencer Award — Yoga & Fitness: Simone Sharice
For a personalized wellness companion that answers, “What should I do today?” Judges will consider how effectively the app turns movement, Pilates, recovery, and other wellness needs into a clear, achievable daily plan without creating more information overload.
Influencer Award — Career Coaching: Leadership Heather
For an app that helps new managers practise difficult workplace conversations before the stakes are real. Judges are looking for realistic scenarios, useful feedback, confidence-building experiences, and active practice with situations such as feedback, boundaries, and saying no.
Influencer Award — Gaming: Mr Lewis Blogs Gaming
For a gaming bucket-list app that makes it easy to save games at the moment of discovery. Judges will consider how effectively users can organize, complete, rate, and share games, along with whether managing a backlog feels enjoyable rather than like another chore.

Ship Kotlin Everywhere — JetBrains
Recognizes a creative, polished app built for both iOS and Android using Kotlin Multiplatform, Compose Multiplatform, or both. Judges will consider cross-platform quality, consistency, and effective use of Kotlin. Optional consideration may be given to useful build content and contributions to the Kotlin Multiplatform community.
Most Viral App — Noise
Awarded to the app that captures the most attention in its market through high-quality, scalable content that converts into downloads. Judges will consider virality, whether the creative formats can be repeated at scale, and how clearly the content communicates the app’s value and addresses users’ needs.
Best App for Galaxy — Samsung
Recognizes a high-quality app published on the Galaxy Store and thoughtfully optimized for Samsung Galaxy users. Judges will consider the overall app experience and Galaxy-specific optimization, including foldable-device support, Samsung features, and device performance. Galaxy Store exclusivity may receive bonus consideration but is not required.
Replit’s Idea to Income
Recognizes a project that uses Replit to turn a new idea into a live, monetizing mobile app. Judges will primarily consider consistent week-over-week growth in revenue and transactions, supported by paying-user traction, product craft, visual design, and meaningful community engagement.
Keep Them Coming Back Award — OneSignal
Recognizes an app that uses OneSignal thoughtfully to bring users back. Judges will consider the quality and stability of the implementation, the value it adds to the user experience, and how creatively the app uses campaigns, push notifications, Journeys, or advanced messaging capabilities.
The Growth Loop Award — Layers
Recognizes a team that treats growth as a process of testing, measuring, learning, and improving. Judges will consider the clarity of the audience and growth hypothesis, how Layers was used to run and observe a focused experiment, whether the SDK was installed correctly, and what the team learned or would try next.
Funnel Vision Award — Stripe
Recognizes a project that launches a web funnel using RevenueCat Funnels and Stripe. Judges will consider the qualifying web payment volume processed during the measurement period and whether the funnel is thoughtfully designed, functional, and effectively implemented.

Requirements
WHAT TO BUILD
Entrants must create a working software application that uses the RevenueCat SDK to power at least one in-app or web purchase (each a "Project"). Apps must be built for iOS, iPadOS, macOS, or Android. The first public version of your app must be released between August 1 and September 30, 2026.

Students competing for the Next Gen Award follow a different, lower-friction path: submit a video and source code instead of a published store listing. No paid Apple or Google developer account required. Full Next Gen rules will be published on this page.

What to submit on Devpost
Every submission must include:

A text description of your app's features and functionality.
A demo video of your project. The video:
Should show the app running on the device it was built for.
Must be no longer than 2 minutes of essential footage — judges are not required to watch beyond two minutes.
Must be uploaded to and publicly visible on YouTube or Vimeo, with the link provided on this form.
Must not include third-party trademarks or copyrighted music/material unless you have permission to use it.
A URL to your fully published app on the App Store or Google Play or Samsung Galaxy Store.
A 1024×1024 app icon.
At least one screenshot of the app at 1179px × 2556px with no device frame.
Either a free trial in your app, or a promo code so judges can unlock the in-app purchase and test all premium features.
